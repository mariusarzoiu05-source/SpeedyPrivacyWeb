05

Data We Collect

Speedy collects the following data to provide its core functionality:

  • Account data — email address, display name
  • Location data — GPS coordinates during active runs (not stored in background)
  • Run data — distance, duration, pace, calories burned, timestamps
  • Preferences — notification settings, training goals, unit preferences
Location access: GPS is only used while you are actively recording a run. We do not track your location in the background or when the App is closed.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in two places:

  • Locally — run history is stored on your device using a local database (Room)
  • Cloud — account information and preferences are stored in Firebase (Google Cloud), secured by Firebase Authentication

You may delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

Your rights (GDPR): If you are located in the European Union, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.
